Rabota2 Studio

A private publishing tool for our own YouTube channels.

Rabota2 Studio is an internal tool used by its owner to prepare animated educational and entertainment videos and upload them to YouTube channels that the owner manages.

Every video is reviewed by a person before it is published. The tool does not publish anything automatically without the owner's approval, and it is not offered to the public.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's channels, and the YouTube Analytics API to read basic statistics of those channels.
