Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Rabota2 Studio ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to YouTube channels that the owner manages. The app is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app receives access through Google OAuth with these permissions:

  • uploading videos, thumbnails and captions to the connected channel;
  • reading the channel name and ID to confirm the right channel is connected;
  • reading YouTube Analytics statistics of the connected channel (views, watch time, retention, subscribers).

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's own videos and to prepare statistics reports for the owner. The app does not use Google user data for advertising and does not sell, rent or transfer it to third parties.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the app.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the app can no longer access the channel.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
